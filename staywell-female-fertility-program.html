A Root-Cause Medicine Program

How the program works and what it costs

The Female Fertility Program is a 3-month program, sold separately from membership. We look for what is affecting your cycles and ovulation, start your plan, and recheck your hormones at month 3 to see what changed.

Every program includes

  • A 1-hour initial visit and 3 monthly follow-up visits
  • A comprehensive baseline blood panel
  • Blood tests to confirm ovulation in your first 3 treatment cycles, and a hormone recheck at month 3
  • 3 months of your core supplement, at a package discount
  • Ovulation medication management when appropriate (the medication itself is paid at the pharmacy)
  • A personalized plan for nutrition, lifestyle, and supplements, with specialist coordination if you need more advanced treatment

Good to know

Not included in the price
Medication such as letrozole (paid directly at the pharmacy), imaging, your partner's semen analysis, additional testing your provider recommends, and supplements beyond your core supplement, which are available at 20% off retail.
Who this is not for
This program is not appropriate for patients who are pregnant, have unexplained abnormal bleeding or a suspected pelvic mass, or have new rapid hair growth or voice changes. Those concerns need a medical evaluation first. If you are 40 or older, we refer you to a reproductive endocrinologist at the start so that care can run alongside the program.
Recent testing
If you have had one of the program labs in the past 90 days, it may be credited toward your baseline panel.
Complete Membership pricing
Requires an active membership through the end of the program. If membership is canceled before completion, the price difference is billed ($790 with labs included, or $425 when using insurance for labs).
Our promise
We work to improve the conditions for ovulation and conception. We cannot guarantee a pregnancy.
A Different Starting Point

Support and coordination, not a fertility clinic

This program is a root-cause workup and support system, not IVF treatment. We look for the hormonal and metabolic patterns that may be affecting your cycles or your chances of conceiving, and we coordinate with a reproductive endocrinologist or fertility specialist when more advanced treatment, like IUI or IVF, is the right next step.

Common reasons to start here

  • Irregular, unpredictable, or absent menstrual cycles
  • A PCOS diagnosis, or symptoms suggestive of PCOS

What to Expect

A process, not a quick fix

1

Intake Consultation

Your provider reviews your cycle history, symptoms, prior testing, and fertility goals to shape a targeted workup.

2

Comprehensive Lab Panel

A thorough hormone and metabolic panel, interpreted in the context of your cycle and symptoms, not just flagged in or out of range.

3

Your Individualized Plan

Based on your labs and goals, your provider builds a plan: ovulation tracking, lifestyle optimization, medication support, or a combination.

4

Follow-Up & Refinement

Monthly check-ins let your provider track your response, confirm ovulation with a blood test when you are on ovulation medication, and adjust your plan. A hormone recheck at month 3 shows how far you have come, and monthly extensions are available if you want to continue.

5

Specialist Coordination, If Needed

If more advanced treatment is the right path, we coordinate with a reproductive endocrinologist or fertility specialist to keep your care connected.
