The Female Fertility Program is sold and priced separately from membership, a 6-month program built around your initial visit plus 6 monthly follow-ups. Complete Members receive a discounted program price; Access Members and non-members pay the standard price.

Your program price is all-inclusive: your consultations, comprehensive hormone and metabolic lab panel, and your core supplement protocol for the full 6 months are covered, no extra charge along the way. A Different Starting Point

Support and coordination, not a fertility clinic

This program is a root-cause workup and support system, not IVF treatment. We look for the hormonal and metabolic patterns that may be affecting your cycles or your chances of conceiving, and we coordinate with a reproductive endocrinologist or fertility specialist when more advanced treatment, like IUI or IVF, is the right next step.

Recognizing the Signs

Common reasons to start here

  • Irregular, unpredictable, or absent menstrual cycles
  • A PCOS diagnosis, or symptoms suggestive of PCOS
  • Unexplained infertility after basic testing came back normal
  • Recurrent early pregnancy loss
  • Wanting a root-cause hormone workup before starting fertility treatment
  • Wanting support alongside treatment you've already started elsewhere
The Staywell Approach

A thorough workup, built around your cycle

We start with the data, then build a plan around what your labs and your history actually show.

Comprehensive Hormone Panel

A thorough hormone and metabolic lab panel to identify patterns affecting ovulation, cycle regularity, and fertility.

Ovulation Tracking Guidance

Practical, individualized guidance on tracking ovulation to better understand your cycle and timing.

Medication Support

Evidence-based medication support when appropriate, such as ovulation induction, guided by your provider.

Individualized Nutrient Support

Your core supplement protocol, covered for the full 6 months, from foundational support to nutrients targeted to your labs, alongside sleep, stress, and lifestyle guidance.

Specialist Coordination

When more advanced treatment like IUI or IVF is the right next step, we coordinate with a reproductive endocrinologist or fertility specialist.

Ongoing Support

Continued monitoring and adjustments as your cycle responds, whether you're working with us alone or alongside a fertility clinic.

What to Expect

A process, not a quick fix

1

Intake Consultation

Your provider reviews your cycle history, symptoms, prior testing, and fertility goals to shape a targeted workup.

2

Comprehensive Lab Panel

A thorough hormone and metabolic panel, interpreted in the context of your cycle and symptoms, not just flagged in or out of range.

3

Your Individualized Plan

Based on your labs and goals, your provider builds a plan: ovulation tracking, lifestyle optimization, medication support, or a combination.

4

Follow-Up & Refinement

Regular check-ins let your provider track your response and adjust your plan as your cycle changes.

5

Specialist Coordination, If Needed

If more advanced treatment is the right path, we coordinate with a reproductive endocrinologist or fertility specialist to keep your care connected.
