Functional Medicine

Root-cause care for your whole health picture

Functional medicine looks past a normal lab range to find out why you don't feel normal, using thorough lab work and a whole-person view to get to what's actually driving your symptoms.

Normal labs don't always mean you feel normal

Conventional medicine often stops at "your labs are normal." Functional medicine asks what's next: if your labs are technically normal but you still don't feel like yourself, what's actually going on, and what can be done about it?

A Different Kind of Care

Functional medicine looks deeper

It doesn't replace your primary care, it expands what's possible within it.

Conventional Model

  • Treat the symptom in front of you
  • Reference ranges built around average, not optimal
  • Rushed visits, limited lab review
  • Refer out when standard testing comes back normal
  • One-size-fits-all treatment plans

The Functional Medicine Approach

  • Look for the root cause, not just symptom relief
  • Interpret labs in context of what's optimal for you
  • Extended visits with real time for a thorough lab review
  • Dig deeper when standard testing comes back normal
  • Protocols personalized to your biology and history
