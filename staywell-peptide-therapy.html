A Note on Off-Label Use

Most of the peptides discussed here are not FDA-approved for the specific use described on this page. Many are prescribed off-label based on clinical evidence, published research, and professional judgment, similar to how a range of medications are used in general practice. Some are supplied through licensed compounding pharmacies rather than as individually FDA-approved drug products, and a few are still classified as investigational. Your provider will walk you through the evidence, the risks, and the alternatives for anything recommended as part of your plan.

GH Secretagogue

Sermorelin / Ipamorelin / CJC-1295

These peptides stimulate your pituitary gland to produce and release your own growth hormone, rather than replacing it directly, for a more physiologic increase in GH and IGF-1.

  • Improved sleep quality and recovery
  • Lean muscle preservation and growth
  • Reduction in visceral fat
  • Enhanced energy and cognitive clarity
Cellular Energy

NAD+ / NMN

NAD+ is a coenzyme essential to cellular energy production and DNA repair. Levels decline significantly with age, and restoring them is a well-researched strategy for cellular longevity.

  • Mitochondrial function and energy production
  • DNA repair and cellular resilience
  • Cognitive function and neuroprotection
  • Metabolic regulation and inflammation reduction
Repair & Recovery

BPC-157

Body Protective Compound 157 is a synthetic peptide derived from a protein found in gastric juice, with tissue-healing and anti-inflammatory properties across multiple systems.

  • Accelerated tendon and ligament repair
  • Gut lining support and GI healing
  • Reduced systemic inflammation
  • Neuroprotective and joint-supportive effects
Tissue Healing

TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

TB-500 promotes the regulation of actin, a protein critical to cell structure and movement, playing a key role in wound healing, muscle repair, and reducing inflammation.

  • Accelerated healing of muscle, tendon, and connective tissue
  • Reduced inflammation at injury sites
  • Improved flexibility and range of motion
  • Often combined with BPC-157 for synergistic repair
Weight Management

Semaglutide & Tirzepatide (GLP-1)

These GLP-1 peptides regulate appetite and blood sugar, supporting sustained, clinician-guided weight loss as part of a broader plan built around your goals.

  • Appetite regulation and reduced cravings
  • Improved blood sugar control
  • Titrated dosing, monitored by your provider
  • Provider-guided under Access, Complete, and Optimization memberships
