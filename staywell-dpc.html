Primary Care Services

What's covered

Comprehensive healthcare designed to support your whole health journey, from prevention to treatment.

Comprehensive Physical Exams

Thorough annual wellness exams and preventive screenings to catch potential issues early.

Chronic Disease Management

Managing conditions like diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease with a focus on lifestyle interventions.

Mental Health Support

Integrated care including anxiety and depression management, stress reduction, and referrals when needed.

Laboratory Services

Labs are billed separately, directly to you or to your insurance if you choose that route. Discounted labs are available to Complete members, with exclusive lab discounts for Optimization members.

Acute Health Needs

Care for acute illnesses and injuries, including sutures, skin lesion removal, and biopsies.

Holistic & Integrative Care

Care that looks at the whole person, nutrition, movement, stress, sleep, to address root causes.

What Makes Us Different

Care built around you, not a schedule

Direct Primary Care means the structure of your visits changes, not just who's paying for them.

Longer Appointments

30 to 60 minute appointments give us time to really understand your health concerns and develop comprehensive treatment plans.

Direct Access

Text or email your provider directly. No waiting on hold, no intermediaries, just direct communication when you need it.

Personalized Care

Treatment plans tailored to your unique needs, preferences, and health goals, not one-size-fits-all protocols.

Using Insurance or Self-Pay Labs

You're welcome to run labs through your insurance if you have it. If you'd rather skip the insurance process, Staywell Health also offers labs at a discounted self-pay rate, ordered and interpreted directly by your provider. We'll go over both options with you before any labs are drawn.
