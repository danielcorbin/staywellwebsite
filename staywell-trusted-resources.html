Trusted Local Resources

Care that complements what happens here

A short list of local professionals and services Daniel personally trusts and refers patients to, for the parts of your health picture that fall outside what we treat directly at Staywell.

Why We Share This

Not an endorsement for a fee, just what we'd tell a friend

These are professionals and businesses in the Raleigh area that Daniel has personally vetted, worked alongside, or sent patients to with good results. Nobody on this list pays to be here.

Chiropractic Care

Angel Hands Chiropractic - Dr. Angel Gaspar

Chiropractic Physician

Dr. Gaspar takes a thorough, individualized approach, combining gentle instrument-assisted techniques (like Activator Methods® and ArthroStim®) with manual adjustments to treat spinal alignment, neck and back pain, TMJ/jaw issues, joint problems, and sports and auto injuries. He takes the time to listen and understand each patient's full history before building a plan around them. Dr. Corbin has personally seen him for neck pain, back pain, and TMJ issues. Accepts most insurance and also offers affordable cash-pay pricing.

Physical Therapy

Restore Physical Therapy and Wellness - Shilpa Kundeshwar, PT

Physical Therapist

Shilpa is a physical therapist with over 30 years of experience, board-certified as an Exercise Expert for Aging Adults, working with patients of all ages and injury types through manual therapy, dry needling, and personalized treatment plans. She also offers in-home mobile services for patients who need care outside the clinic. Dr. Corbin has worked with her for neck pain and experienced significant relief.

Local Point Concierge Physical Therapy & Wellness - Dr. Christie Kornegay, PT, DPT

Doctor of Physical Therapy

Dr. Kornegay brings over 15 years of orthopedic experience to a true one-on-one concierge model, no aides, no crowded clinic, with longer appointments and hands-on techniques including dry needling, cupping, therapeutic massage, and instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization. She treats TMJ disorders, postpartum recovery, and sports injuries, with both in-clinic and home visit options available. Dr. Corbin has firsthand experience with dry needling for TMJ jaw pain and saw significant improvement.

Integrative Wellness & Thermography

Denise Souza Integrative Wellness - Denise Souza

Thermography & Integrative Wellness

Denise takes a holistic and integrative approach to wellness, focused on what she calls "nurturing the soil," addressing the underlying terrain of the body, mind, and spirit rather than chasing symptoms in isolation. She specializes in thermography, a full-body infrared imaging technique that screens for early signs of inflammation and other changes without radiation, compression, or contact, and also offers food sensitivity testing, blood cell analysis, and other integrative wellness services.
