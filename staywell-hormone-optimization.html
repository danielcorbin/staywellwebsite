A Familiar Frustration

Your labs came back "normal." But you don't feel normal.

Standard reference ranges are built around population averages, not optimal function. A TSH of 3.8 is technically within range, but for many people it represents sluggish thyroid function that affects energy, weight, and mood. At Staywell, labs are read the way they were meant to be, in the context of your symptoms, your history, and what optimal actually looks like for you.

Recognizing the Signs

Is this you?

Hormonal shifts rarely announce themselves clearly. It's usually a slow drift away from who you used to be, until one day something feels off.

Common symptoms worth a closer look

  • Low energy and motivation, even with adequate sleep
  • Declining strength or difficulty building muscle
  • Reduced libido or erectile dysfunction
  • Brain fog and poor concentration
  • Increased body fat, especially visceral
  • Mood changes: irritability, low drive, flat affect
  • Poor sleep quality or insomnia
  • Hair loss or skin changes
  • Andropause, a gradual hormonal decline with age

Common symptoms worth a closer look

  • Fatigue that sleep doesn't fix
  • Irregular, heavy, or painful periods
  • Perimenopause or menopause symptoms: hot flashes, night sweats, mood shifts
  • Brain fog and difficulty concentrating
  • Low libido or vaginal dryness
  • Unexplained weight gain, especially around the midsection
  • Anxiety, irritability, or low mood
  • Hair thinning or skin changes
  • Thyroid symptoms: cold intolerance, constipation, fatigue
The Staywell Approach

A supervised, evolving process

We don't hand you a standard protocol and send you on your way. Hormone optimization here is built around your individual response, not a fixed dose.

Expert Lab Interpretation

A comprehensive hormone and micronutrient panel, read against optimal ranges, not just population averages, in the context of your symptoms and goals.

Bioidentical Hormone Therapy

Hormones molecularly identical to what your body produces, compounded to your precise dose. No one-size-fits-all prescriptions.

Micronutrient Optimization

Hormones don't work in isolation. We assess and address the magnesium, zinc, B vitamins, and vitamin D that hormonal systems depend on.

Ongoing Monitoring

Regular follow-up labs and check-ins keep your protocol working and allow for precise adjustments as your body responds.

Direct Provider Access

Text your provider between visits with questions or symptom changes. You won't wait weeks for an answer when something feels off.

Whole-Person Context

Sleep, stress, nutrition, and lifestyle all shape hormone function, and all factor into your protocol, not just the numbers on a lab report.
