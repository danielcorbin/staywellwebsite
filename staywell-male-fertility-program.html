The Staywell Approach

Testing and optimization, built around your results

We start with a clear picture, then build a plan to support sperm health from there.

Semen Analysis

A baseline semen analysis to assess count, motility, and morphology, giving you a clear starting picture.

Male Hormone Panel

A comprehensive panel including testosterone, LH, and FSH, interpreted in the context of your symptoms and goals.

Individualized Nutrient Support

Your core supplement protocol, covered for the full 3 months, from foundational support to nutrients targeted to your labs or semen analysis, not a one-size-fits-all bottle of vitamins.

Diet & Lifestyle Foundation

Practical guidance on diet, exercise, sleep, and heat or alcohol exposure, the everyday factors that measurably affect sperm health and testosterone production.

Follow-Up & Retesting

Repeat testing to track your response and confirm whether your protocol is moving the numbers in the right direction.

Specialist Coordination

When more advanced treatment is needed, we coordinate with a fertility specialist to keep your care connected.
