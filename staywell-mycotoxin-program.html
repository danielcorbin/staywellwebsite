How the program works and what it costs

The Mycotoxin & Mold Illness Program is a 3-month program, sold separately from membership. We look for the source of your exposure, build your energy, antioxidant capacity, and gut clearance first, and add a binder only when your body is ready for it.

Every program includes

  • A 1-hour workup visit and two 30-minute follow-up visits
  • A baseline lab panel to check that your body can safely support detoxification
  • A baseline urine MycoTOX Profile to measure your mycotoxin burden
  • An EMMA environmental dust test to help identify the source of exposure
  • 3 months of your core supplements, at a package discount
  • A step-by-step plan that sequences your gut, bile flow, and binder, with binder prescription management (the binder itself is filled at your pharmacy)

An Overlooked Cause

When symptoms don't add up, the environment might be the missing piece

Chronic exposure to mold or water-damaged buildings can drive a wide, seemingly unrelated set of symptoms that standard workups often miss. If you've been told your labs are normal but you still don't feel like yourself, and you suspect your home, workplace, or a past exposure may be part of the picture, this program is built to investigate that possibility directly.

Recognizing the Signs

Is this you?

Mold-related illness rarely presents as one clear symptom. It's usually a cluster of issues that don't fully explain themselves.

Common symptoms worth a closer look

  • Persistent fatigue that doesn't improve with rest
  • Brain fog, poor concentration, or word-finding trouble
  • Chronic sinus congestion or respiratory irritation
  • Joint pain or muscle aches without a clear cause
  • Frequent headaches
  • Increasing sensitivity to chemicals, scents, or environmental triggers
  • Symptoms that improve when away from a particular building
  • A known history of water damage at home or work
The Staywell Approach

A structured path to clearance

This program moves in a deliberate sequence, from identifying exposure to confirming your body has recovered.

Exposure History Intake

A detailed review of your home, workplace, and any known water damage or mold exposure, alongside your symptom timeline.

Mycotoxin Testing

A baseline urine MycoTOX Profile to help identify which mycotoxins may be present and driving your symptoms.

Personalized Binder Protocol

A sequenced elimination protocol built around your results. Binders are started only once your gut, bile flow, and detox pathways are ready for them, so you are not asked to mobilize more than your body can clear.

Individualized Nutrient Support

Your core supplement protocol, with your first 3 months included, plus nutrients targeted to your results to support gut and sinus recovery from the inside.

Environmental Guidance

An EMMA environmental dust test helps identify mycotoxins in your home or workplace, with practical guidance on reducing ongoing exposure, coordinated with your own remediation efforts. Staywell does not perform remediation itself.

Follow-Up Retesting

Optional repeat MycoTOX testing to document clearance and guide whether your protocol needs adjustment as you progress.
