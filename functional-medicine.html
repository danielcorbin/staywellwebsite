Normal Labs Don't Always Mean You're Functioning Well

Standard primary care labs are built to catch disease, not to optimize function. Functional medicine looks further upstream, at gut health, inflammation, nutrient status, hormonal balance, and metabolic patterns, to find out why you feel the way you feel, even when your labs come back "normal."

What Functional Medicine Includes

This is a deeper level of workup and management than a standard visit is designed for, built for patients who want a root-cause approach.

Comprehensive Functional Lab Panel

An expanded panel covering gut health, inflammatory markers, nutrient status, metabolic function, and hormonal patterns, well beyond a standard primary care workup.

Root-Cause Interpretation

Your provider reads your results in the context of your full history and symptoms, looking for patterns and underlying drivers rather than treating each marker in isolation.

Personalized Protocol

A plan built around what your labs and history actually show, which may include nutrition and lifestyle changes, targeted supplementation, or medication where appropriate.

Ongoing Monitoring

Functional medicine is a process. Follow-up labs and visits track your response and allow your provider to refine your protocol over time.

Direct Provider Access

Text your provider between visits with questions about your protocol, symptoms, or progress.

Who Functional Medicine Is For

This isn't for everyone, and that's by design. It's built for patients who want a deeper, more involved level of investigation and management.

  • Unexplained fatigue, brain fog, or digestive symptoms that haven't responded to standard care
  • Chronic inflammation or autoimmune concerns you want investigated further
  • A history of being told your labs are "normal" without a clear explanation for how you feel
  • Interest in a root-cause, whole-person approach rather than symptom management alone
  • Willingness to invest the time in comprehensive testing, protocol changes, and ongoing follow-up
  • Patients already familiar with Staywell through primary care or another specialty program

What to Expect

Functional medicine is a supervised, iterative process. Here is what the journey typically looks like.

1

In-Depth Intake

Your provider reviews your full health history, symptoms, prior labs, and goals in detail. This conversation shapes which functional markers are worth investigating for you specifically.

2

Comprehensive Functional Lab Panel

A broad panel is ordered based on your intake, often covering gut health, inflammatory markers, nutrient status, hormones, and metabolic function.

3

Root-Cause Review and Protocol Design

Your provider interprets your results in context and builds a personalized protocol, which may include lifestyle changes, targeted supplementation, or medication.

4

Implementation and Follow-Up

You begin the protocol with your provider tracking your response, answering questions, and making adjustments as needed.

5

Long-Term Optimization

Once your symptoms and markers stabilize, the focus shifts to maintaining your progress with periodic monitoring.

Using Insurance or Self-Pay Labs
